A biscuit-making workshop (taller de galletas) inspired by the barraquito, the layered coffee drink you'll find in every bar on the island. It's part of the fifth Ruta del Barraquito, which the council says it will announce in more detail soon, and is run with the local traders' association ASEMTEIDE.

Places must be booked in advance by phone on 922 86 31 27, extension 4050. The announcement is in Spanish only.
