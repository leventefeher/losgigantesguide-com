The fifth edition of the Santiago del Teide padel tournament, run by the council with Banco Santander Agentes Acantilados de Los Gigantes and Landmar Hotels. Matches are played on the courts of the Complejo Deportivo Pancracio Socas García, with second, third, fourth and mixed categories.

There's no published daily match schedule, and the council and Canary Run give different start and finish times. If you want to watch, ask at the sports complex which days have matches and when the finals are.

Registration is open on Canary Run (in Spanish). Questions go to the council's sports department on 922 86 81 08.
