The fifth edition of the Santiago del Teide padel tournament, run by the council with Banco Santander Agentes Acantilados de Los Gigantes and Landmar Hotels. Matches are played on the courts of the Complejo Deportivo Pancracio Socas García, with second, third, fourth and mixed categories.

The council lists it as running from 08:00 on 9 November to 17:00 on 21 November, but hasn't published a daily match schedule. If you want to watch, ask at the sports complex which days have matches and when the finals are.

Registration is open through Canary Run, according to the council's announcement (in Spanish).
