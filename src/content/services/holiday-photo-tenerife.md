Bálint has been photographing couples and families in Tenerife since 2018, and he works in several languages. Sessions are built around you rather than a fixed script, and he does plenty of the directing so you're not left wondering what to do with your hands.

## Sessions

- **Couples:** golden-hour shoots, good for honeymoons and anniversaries
- **Families:** relaxed and playful, aimed at capturing everyone as they actually are
- **Solo travellers:** a portrait session planned entirely around you

## Locations

He shoots across the island, including right here on the Los Gigantes cliffs, plus the El Médano dunes, the colonial streets of La Laguna, and Playa del Duque in the south. You settle on the spot that suits the photos you want.

## Packages

Pricing is published up front:

- **Essential, €220:** 1 hour, one location, 20 edited photos
- **Experience, €350:** 2–3 hours, two locations, 40 edited photos
- **Deluxe, €490:** 4–6 hours, multiple locations, 60 edited photos

## Practical tips

- Message on WhatsApp to check dates and talk through what you want before booking
- For outdoor sessions, golden hour is the goal, so be ready for an early-morning or late-afternoon slot
- The Essential package is the one most people pick if you just want a solid set of photos from the trip
