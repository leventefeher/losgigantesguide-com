Roll With It is Mark and Jo, a small, personal option for keeping a home or holiday let in shape. They cover the south of Tenerife, including Los Gigantes, and quotes and advice are free.

## What they do

- **Painting and varnishing**, inside and out
- **Terrace balustrades**: fitting new ones, or repairing spindles and rails on existing ones
- **Tiling and re-grouting** floors, terraces and balconies, inside and out
- **Basic plumbing**
- **General maintenance** around the property

## Practical tips

- Send a few photos on WhatsApp first (the cracked grout, the peeling wall, the wobbly balustrade) so they can give you a rough idea before visiting
- For bigger plumbing jobs, ask whether it's within what they cover, as they list basic plumbing only
- If you're away for part of the year, it's worth asking about regular upkeep while you're gone
