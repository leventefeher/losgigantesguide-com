A small studio in the town itself, on Avenida Quinto Centenario, running group classes in small groups: functional circuits, HIIT, Tabata and suspension training, plus Pilates and spinal yoga. Sessions are coached, and adapted for everyone from beginners to competitive athletes.

The training side is run by Tamas Kulifai, an Olympic silver medallist and world champion in kayaking with a master's in sports coaching. Marietta Toth teaches the yoga and mobility classes.

## The classes

- **G-Flex suspension training:** their own method using suspension straps, so you're constantly working against instability. Good for balance and core as much as strength. All levels.
- **Functional circuits and Tabata:** each runs in a moderate and a high-intensity version. Tabata is 20 seconds of work, 10 seconds of rest, on repeat. If you're new, start with the moderate one.
- **HIIT:** the hardest of the lot, with explosive exercises and short rests.
- **Pilates:** check the weekly timetable for when it's on.
- **Spinal yoga and mobility:** beginners' and advanced classes on weekdays, guided meditation at weekends, and candlelight yoga in the evenings. A good counterweight to the harder sessions, or for loosening up after the flight.

On Sundays they run a free community hike, open to all levels. Message them on WhatsApp to be added to the group chat.

They also do one-to-one personal training and a nutrition coaching programme (around four months; the first consultation is free).

## Booking and prices

The weekly timetable is posted on their [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/tenerife.movement/) and their [Google Maps listing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gL9J2dWvjfJuwVNW8), not on the website. The easiest way to book is WhatsApp on +34 660 713 295. You can also email info@tenerifemovement.com or use the contact form on their website.

If you're trying it out, the Welcome Pass gets you 4 group classes for €35, and you can mix different class types to find the one that suits you.

## Practical tips

- **Book before you turn up.** Groups are kept small, so don't count on walking in.
- **The website is in Spanish only.** Your browser's translate button handles it fine.
- **Bring water and a towel**, especially for the high-intensity classes.

## What I'd skip

Going straight into the high-intensity Tabata or HIIT on your first day, especially in summer. Do the moderate version first and see how you handle the heat.
